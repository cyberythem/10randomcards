# 10 Random Cards 🃏✨

**Stop doom-scrolling and start clicking.**

I built a digital playground that refuses to be boring. Inside are 10 distinct, unpredictable, and slightly chaotic experiments that have absolutely no business being on the same page—and yet, here they are.

Some will help you make decisions. Some will give you excuses. Some will just make you laugh at how useless they are. 

### Why are you here? 
You’re curious. You want to see what happens when you press a button without knowing the outcome. You want to see if your luck holds up or if your screen is about to reveal something completely unexpected.

Don't read about it. Don't analyze it. **Just go.**

### [👉 Step into the Playground: 10randomcards.vercel.app](https://10randomcards.vercel.app)

---

### What’s the catch?
There isn't one. It’s just 10 different ways to lose track of time. Whether you’re trying to win an argument, generate a bizarre content idea, or just need a D20 roll to decide your next move—the answers are waiting.

**Warning:** *You might stay longer than you intended.*

* **Built with**: HTML5, CSS3 (Liquid Glassmorphism), and Vanilla JavaScript.
* **The Vibe**: Chaotic, responsive, and intentionally mysterious.
